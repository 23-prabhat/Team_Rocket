3. Misinformation Detection in Regional Languages 
Problem Statement: 
Design a system that detects fake or misleading content in regional Indian languages and 
explains why it is flagged. 
Description: 
Fake news spreads rapidly, especially in regional languages where detection tools are limited. 
The system should analyze input content, identify whether it is misleading or false, and 
provide a simple explanation for its decision. The focus should be on handling at least one 
regional language effectively and making the output understandable for users.
